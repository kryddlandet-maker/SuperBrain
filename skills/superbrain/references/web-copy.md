# Web copy grounded in the actual product

Use for website copy, microcopy and rewrites. Start from the visitor's task, verified facts and supplied voice samples. State what the product helps someone do, how it works and the real next action. Put evidence close to the claim it supports.

Review whole passages before individual words: repetitive heading shapes, empty importance claims, staged introductions and a conclusion that repeats the paragraph often add length without information. Replace them when they obscure the actual message. Preserve deliberate voice, quoted text, technical terms, uncertainty, numbers, dates and claim qualifications. Punctuation and familiar fonts are not evidence that writing is bad or AI-generated.

Separate observed customer language from an authored positioning hypothesis. Interview observations retain their source and contradictory evidence; do not manufacture quotations or participant counts to fill a template. Missing proof remains missing. Testimonials, guarantees and performance claims need supplied support.

Button labels describe the real action and destination. Loading, empty, error and success text must describe actual state and give a recovery action where possible. A stored enquiry is not a confirmed booking. Do not rewrite legal text or translate claims without the requested scope and review.

Re-render after copy edits: inspect heading wraps, long Swedish words, prices and names at narrow widths. The copy pass should preserve meaning while improving readability; it is not an AI detector or a mandatory casual tone.
