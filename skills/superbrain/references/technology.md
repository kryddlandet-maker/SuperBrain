# Expressive technology sites

Use the product's actual purpose as the visual subject. A workflow diagram, collaboration scene or real product view can carry a more specific idea than generic glass cards. The hero should state the value while the imagery invites exploration.

Huly is a reference for luminous atmosphere, product staging and interactive detail, not a required palette or structure. Preserve the target brand's voice. A cursor reveal can expose secondary decorative fragments while the main explanation is always readable.

Give product, solutions, pricing and resources distinct routes where content warrants them. A marketing website is not the entire application: do not imply that a demo dashboard is a working product. Connect trial/demo actions to the real destination or visibly mark a prototype.

Design spatial relationships: background atmosphere, content plane, foreground object, stable controls. Use depth selectively. Fine borders, sparse glows and a controlled accent can establish continuity without a forest of panels.

Prototype a signature interaction alongside the hero. Then examine mid-page rhythm and inner-page layouts. Rich motion may occupy several related moments when the narrative supports it; it is not limited by a fixed effect count. Preserve settled reading zones and measure the actual runtime.
